Olivet newspaper · July 8, 1909 (date handwritten on the clipping)

David J. Ripley dies at Olivet

David’s obituary says he had lived in Olivet for about three years after leaving his farm in Brookfield Township, and had suffered from rheumatism for years. His funeral was held at the Townline Church and he was buried in Perry Cemetery.

It gives his birth as 13 September 1851 in Ohio, and says he left a widow and eight children out of a family of twelve.

Full text (published 1909)

Mr. David J. Ripley, who has been a resident of Olivet for some three years, moving here from his farm in Brookfield township, died Tuesday morning about 9 o’clock. He had been a great sufferer for some years from rheumatism. The funeral was held this morning at the Townline Church at 11 o’clock, Rev. Akin conducting the services, the interment in the Perry cemetery. Mr. Ripley was born in Ohio, Sept. 13th, 1851. He leaves a widow and eight children of a family of twelve.
