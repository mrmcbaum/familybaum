Michigan marriage records · Eaton County return for 1877, no. 220

Marriage of David J. Ripley and Elizabeth L. Krebs, 14 January 1877

David, 24, of Carmel, and Elizabeth, 17, of Brookfield, were married in Charlotte by a justice of the peace. Two Ripleys from Walton, Sylvester and Elizabeth A., were the witnesses.

Michigan death certificate · Eaton County, 1909

Death of David J. Ripley, 6 July 1909

David died at Olivet, aged 57. The certificate says he was the father of 12 children, 8 of them living, and gives his parents as Manford Ripley of New York and Mary Pelton of Ohio.

Elizabeth as a widow, 1910
