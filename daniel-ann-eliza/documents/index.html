Eaton County return of marriages for 1874 · page 249, entry 42

Daniel & Ann Eliza’s marriage record

Daniel Baum, 20, a farmer born in Brookfield, married Ann Eliza Britton, 27, born in Ohio, on 18 January 1874 at Brookfield, Eaton County. A justice of the peace performed the ceremony, and two Brookfield neighbours witnessed it.

State of Michigan certificate of death · Walton Township, Eaton County · 1914

Ann Eliza’s death certificate

Ann Eliza died at home in Walton Township on 19 August 1914, aged 67. Her husband Daniel gave the information. The certificate names her parents as Abraham K. Britton of Ohio and Elizabeth Golden of New York, and she was buried at Eaton Rapids.

Michigan Department of Health certificate of death · Lee Township, Calhoun County · 1932

Daniel’s death certificate

Daniel died on 1 May 1932 in Lee Township, Calhoun County, aged 79, a retired farmer and widower of Emma Baum. His son, listed as “Mr. Jay Baum” of Olivet, gave the information. The certificate names his parents as John Baum and Mary Harshey (Hershey), both born in Ohio. He was buried at Hamlin.
