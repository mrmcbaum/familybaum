Certified copy of record of birth · Eaton County Clerk, Charlotte, Michigan

Dott's birth record

Dott Ripley, a girl, was born on 23 February 1886 in Brookfield Township, Eaton County. Her father was David J. Ripley, born in Ohio, and her mother was Elizabeth E. Kreps (Krebs), born in Michigan, who was living in Brookfield. The birth was recorded on 15 June 1887 (Liber 3, page 400). The certified copy was issued in 2010.

Eaton County marriage register, 1903 · two facing pages, record no. 174

J. D. & Dott's marriage record

J. D. Baum, 21, a farmer of Walton, son of Daniel Baum, married Dott Ripley, 18, of Lee, daughter of David Ripley. Both were born in Michigan, and this was the first marriage for each.

The register names their mothers as Ann Brinton (Britten) and Elizabeth Krebs. They married on 16 August 1903 in Walton, Michigan, before E. A. Olmstead, Minister of the Gospel. The witnesses were Andrew Baum of Brookfield and Pheba Ripley of Lee.

U.S. World War I Draft Registration Card · 12 September 1918

World War I draft registration

J. D. was 36, born 8 August 1882, and farming for himself in Lee, Calhoun County, with his home at Olivet. His nearest relative was his wife, Dott Baum.

The registrar described him as of medium height and medium build, with blue eyes and brown hair. The card was registered at Marshall, Michigan.

U.S. World War II Draft Registration Card · 27 April 1942

World War II draft registration

J. D. was 59, living at RFD No. 1, Olivet, in Calhoun County, with no telephone. He was self-employed, born 8 August 1882 in Eaton County, and Dott Baum was the person who would always know his address.

The registrar described him as 5 ft 8 in, 165 lb, with blue eyes and gray hair. The card is signed "J. D. Baum."

Michigan Certificate of Death · Eaton County, 1962

Dott's death certificate

Dott Baum, a married housewife, died at 7 p.m. on 15 February 1962 at Eaton Rapids Community Hospital, aged 75. She was born on 23 February 1886 in Eaton County, and her home was R.F.D. 2, Parma, in Springport, Jackson County.

The certificate names her father as David Ripley, her mother as Libbie Krebs and her husband as J. D. Baum. Her daughter, Mrs. Bernice Piepkow of Springport, gave the information. She was buried on 18 February 1962 at Oakridge Cemetery, Marshall, with John M. Kerwin of Springport as funeral director.
