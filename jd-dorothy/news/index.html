Battle Creek Enquirer · Tuesday, 10 April 1945, page 10

Auction Sale

J. D. had sold his farm 10 miles north of Marshall and auctioned off its stock and tools on Thursday, 12 April 1945. The list included three horses, cows and calves, heifers, two bulls, a binder, a hay loader, plows and a potato digger.

Jackson Citizen Patriot · Wednesday, 12 August 1953, page 17

Open House Sunday Will Mark J. D. Baums' 50th Anniversary

The Baums held an open house at their home on Town Road near Springport for their golden anniversary. They had married on 16 August 1903 in Walton Township and farmed at Lee Center from 1906 until about 1946.

At the time they had 30 grandchildren and eight great-grandchildren. J. D. was working at Brown's orchard near Parma and was well known for playing his mouth organ and bones in amateur shows. Both had been Grange members for 37 years, and Dott had belonged to the Maccabees for 25.

Battle Creek Enquirer · Wednesday, 26 August 1953, page 12

Fiftieth Wedding Date Marked at Springport

All of their children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren came for a potluck dinner. 275 relatives and friends came to the open house from towns across Calhoun, Eaton and Jackson counties.

Jackson Citizen Patriot · Thursday, 3 August 1961, page 2

Most Descendants

At the Citizen Patriot Golden Wedding Club picnic, J. D. and Dott won a prize for having the most descendants: 63 in all, made up of six children, 20 grandchildren and 37 great-grandchildren.

Newspaper clipping · dated by hand 17 February 1962

Obituary: Mrs. J. D. Baum

Dott, 75, wife of J. D. Baum of Route 2, Parma, died on Thursday night (15 February 1962) at Community Hospital in Eaton Rapids. She was born in Brookfield Township, Eaton County, the daughter of David and Libbie (Krebs) Ripley. She belonged to the Golden Age Club of Albion, the Maccabees and the Devereaux, state and national granges.

She was survived by her husband, three daughters (Bernice Piepkow, Inez Fetters and Irene Peck) and three sons (Gaylord, Eugene and Harold). She also left 20 grandchildren and 41 great-grandchildren, three sisters (Flora Waytalese of Battle Creek, Stella Smith of Charlotte and Opal Bradley of Jackson) and a brother, Harry Ripley of Olivet.

Jackson Citizen Patriot · Friday, 16 December 1966, page 10

Sunday Rites

Services for J. D., 84, were set for 2 p.m. Sunday at Springport, where he had farmed for several years before moving to Marshall. He belonged to the Devereaux Grange, the Golden Age Club of Albion and the Citizen Patriot Golden Wedding Club.

Jackson Citizen Patriot · Saturday, 17 December 1966, page 10

Obituary: Baum, J. D.

J. D. lived on Route 3, Marshall, and died at Eaton Rapids Hospital at 84. He was survived by his three sons (Gaylord D., Eugene P. and Harold J.), his three daughters (Bernice, Irene and Inez), 20 grandchildren and 57 great-grandchildren.

The Reverend Charles Baum officiated at Kerwin Chapel in Springport, and burial was at Oakridge Cemetery, Marshall.
