A DNA Reconstruction Project of the ancestors and descendants of Gaylord & Arda Baum, their cousins and kin.

Click on Gaylord & Arda's photo to see their family information.

Gaylord and Arda never took a DNA test. Their descendants carry the pieces, and by comparing our results we are rebuilding a DNA profile for each of them, and from theirs, for the couples above. If you found us through an Ancestry match, you probably carry some of those pieces too. You're very welcome here.

Three ways to take part

Any one of these helps, and none costs anything but a little time. Your results stay private to the project: we never publish living people's DNA or names, and you can withdraw at any time.

Share your Ancestry matches

Add Michael as a Collaborator on your AncestryDNA results so he can see your match list and shared matches. You can remove access whenever you like.

  1. In AncestryDNA, open Settings.
  2. Under DNA sharing, invite mrmcbaum@icloud.com.
  3. Choose Collaborator.

Upload to GEDmatch

GEDmatch is free and shows where on each chromosome you match, which is what the rebuild needs most.

  1. Download your raw DNA file from AncestryDNA.
  2. Upload it to a free GEDmatch account.
  3. Email Michael your kit number.

Ask an older relative

A parent, aunt, uncle or grandparent carries twice as much of these ancestors as you do. One test in an older generation can fill large gaps.
